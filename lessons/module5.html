Module 5: Girls Hack

Overview

In this session, teams will be presented with various Hackathon challenges related to the Coding for Confidence themes that they can pick from. They will also be given a bag and other items with the Coding for Confidence logo. The teams will then work on their Hackathon project for the majority of the session. Finally, they will be given more time to work on their binary bracelets, featuring their initials in binary.
