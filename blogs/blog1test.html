Blog 1: Girls Support Girls

Photos

Today's session was a lot of fun! We got to meet the girls and learn a bit more about them through an icebreaker activity. The girls each got a card with a question to ask each other and went around talking to each other. This made it easy for them to find a partner to sit with and do a pair programming challenge. The girls got their first taste of pair programming by working together to design a girl power logo! They came up with some really cool designs that used a lot of different things they liked and had learned from a short introduction about the importance of teamwork and empowerment in programming. Then, the girls got a chance to learn a little bit about programming languages and how they work before doing their first program in JavaScript. It was challenging and they found a lot of errors in their code, but they learned how to solve them and it was exciting to see their reactions to making a successful number guessing game!
