Blog 2: Girls Hack

Today, the girls began their Hackathon! The session started with a Challenge Launch to explain their objective. Then, the girls got time to brainstorm with their group, tasked with making a girl coding club website or an inspiring quotes website. They also got to take some time to work on their binary bracelets, which said Coding for Confidence, along with their initials spelled out in binary! The girls then got their "swag bags" with Coding for Confidence t-shirts, bags, and bracelets! They were very excited to have them. Then, the girls got to work on their projects, practicing their teamwork and coding skills while getting a chance to be creative too. They were all so excited to bring together everything they had learned and the Hackathon was a huge success!
