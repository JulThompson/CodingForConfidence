Blog 3: Girls Just Wanna Have Fun

The girls wrapped up their Hackathon projects today. They started the session by adding the finishing touches to their projects. It was really fun getting to walk around the room and look at all of the great websites they were able to make in a short period of time! I was super proud of all the hard work the girls put in and could tell the girls were proud of the work too. Then, the girls were each given a certificate recognizing them for a skill they had shown throughout the Coding for Confidence experience. The girls were very supportive of one another and cheered for every single award. I really enjoyed seeing the supportive community that had been fostered throughout the project. Then, the girls got to have some fun, eating snacks and celebrating the experience. This was an amazing close to the sessions and I could tell the girls were just as disappointed as I was that it was ending.
